#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The sentences are written for this buyer. The layout could be lifted onto any solo consultant.

The H1 in `components/landing/Hero.tsx` is specific: "AI that keeps your trucks, kitchens, and sites running." The published price and the one-person voice match `PRODUCT.md`. Around that, the page is a familiar studio skeleton: sticky logo nav, centered hero, four icon cards, a property strip, a numbered 2×2, a 1–2–3 process, a founder block, an accordion, and a rounded close. Lucide icons carry no truck, kitchen, or job-site character. The same four jobs are told twice, and `SubBrandsStrip` inserts "The Dobeu Universe" between the tellings. Section headlines slide back to the category: "Four things, done well — built on partnership." and "Let's build the thing."

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect` on `components/landing`, `app/page.tsx`, and `components/CookieBanner.tsx` exited 0 and returned `[]`. No rule hits, no file locations. The detector did not see the compositional problems. That is a clean scan, not evidence the page is finished.

**Visual overlays.** Mutation works in the page. `http://localhost:8400/detect.js` is blocked by the content security policy in `next.config.ts` (`script-src` does not allow that host). No Impeccable overlay rendered. `window.impeccableScan` stayed undefined. There is no user-visible overlay to look at.

#### Overall Impression

The sale is finally in the first viewport. Price, "Book a call," and "Send the job" are on screen at both sizes before any scroll. The page then spends the click, the next scroll, and the ending undoing that clarity. The single biggest opportunity is to make "Send the job" open the promise already printed under the buttons.

#### What's Working

1. **The price is in the hero, in type, before the ask.** `[data-testid="hero-price-line"]` renders "$5k–$30k typical engagement." It is fully inside both viewports (desktop y=628–648, mobile y=521–541). `FinalCTA.tsx` repeats it.
2. **The hint under the hero buttons tells the truth.** "I'll reply with a price band. Not an instant quote." is visible on both viewports, directly under the pair. The dialog is what breaks it.
3. **"Three steps. No theater."** in `HowItWorks.tsx` is the section that sounds like the product: 30-minute discovery, a one-pager within 48 hours, ship in 2–6 weeks, and "If I'm booked, I'll tell you."

#### Priority Issues

**[P0] The commitment buttons change their promise.**
- **Why it matters:** "Send the job" is the success path. The click opens three tabs and a Typeform whose visible copy is "Scope your project and get a planning estimate" and "we generate a preliminary estimate." That is a different product from a price band answered by Jeremy. "Book a call" shares the same dialog, so the two offers collapse.
- **Fix:** "Send the job" opens one short form, and the confirmation repeats the hero hint. Keep "Book a call" as the scheduler, or the fallback form, without a third tab on that entry. Change the public form title so it does not promise a generated estimate.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] On a phone, the cookie bar covers "Dispatch that runs," and after the hero the only sticky action is "Book a call."**
- **Why it matters:** The home case is the first card. At 390×844 its title sits at y=798–818, inside the cookie bar (y=707–844). Hero bottom padding is below the cards, so it does not lift this card. Once `#hero-ctas` leaves, `StickyMobileCTA.tsx` shows only "Book a call," stacked on the 137px cookie bar (about 211px of chrome). There is a stretch where the hero buttons are under the 61px header and the sticky bar has not mounted yet.
- **Fix:** Lift the capability list above the cookie bar. Make the cookie primary quieter than "Book a call." Put "Send the job" on the sticky bar, and hide that bar when the final CTA is on screen.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] The scroll lamp makes ordinary rows look selected.**
- **Why it matters:** `.scroll-lamp` in `components/motion/RouteLight.tsx` and `app/globals.css` is a fixed violet wash under the header. On the live page it crosses "Dispatch that runs," "Scoped proposal," the FAQ row "Do you do retainers?", and the footer identity line. It reads as a selected state, not as light.
- **Fix:** Keep the lamp off body text, or drop it to a edge glow that never sits on a line of copy.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

**[P1] The proof on screen is harder to check than the proof already written.**
- **Why it matters:** A skeptical buyer looks for something they can open. The hero chip says "Stripe-verified." The founder stats are "2019," "NYC," and "Stripe." The shipped-work sentence names Monty AI, Unified AI v1, StatMiner, DTS Contract Engine, IT Consult Funnel, and LastPlate, and links none of them. `TRUST_POSITION` in `lib/jeremy-data.ts` already says to check GitHub, the published prices, and Stripe, and it is not rendered. Missing testimonials are correct. The page still needs a checkable object.
- **Fix:** Render that trust line in place of the stat row and the "Stripe-verified" chip. Link the shipped names that have real repos. Leave metrics unpublished until `approved` is set.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] The four jobs are told twice, and an agency frame sits on top of them.**
- **Why it matters:** Hero cards and `Services.tsx` repeat "Dispatch that runs," "The tool you don't have," "A front door people trust," and "Invoices that go out." Between them, "The Dobeu Universe" asks the visitor to care about dobeu.space and GitHub. The services heading is "Four things, done well — built on partnership," and the intro hires "a long-term IT advisor." Card 04 says this is not a traffic dashboard, then ends on "Lifecycle email, attribution, and the reporting you can check."
- **Fix:** One four-up, in the services grid. Move the universe row to the footer. Rewrite those two headlines in the same voice as the H1.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

The logo in `components/brand/DobeuMark.tsx` stays. None of these fixes redraw it.

#### Persona Red Flags

**Hurried dispatcher.** The H1 and the price work. They hit "Send the job." The failure is the Typeform panel: "About 5 minutes… we generate a preliminary estimate." If they scroll instead, the universe strip arrives before the services grid. After the hero leaves, the phone offers only "Book a call."

**First-time kitchen owner.** The H1 included kitchens, so they stay. The next line fails them: "I ship Dispatch that actually dispatches." Service 01 then says "Claude, Composio, and MCP when the loop needs tools." The FAQ fails them again at "Does the Ops Teardown fee count toward the build?"

**Skeptical buyer.** They look for something they can check. The failure is the founder stat row in `Founder.tsx`: "2019," "NYC," and "Stripe," then six product names with no links. The footer offers "Case studies" with no proof module on this page.
