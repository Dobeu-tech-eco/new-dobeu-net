## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The page is authored in one sentence and interchangeable after it. "AI that keeps your trucks, kitchens, and sites running." plus the diagnostic about spreadsheets is written for a dispatcher or a kitchen owner. The mark (indigo lobes, amber lens, lowercase dobeu.net) is a real identity and it stays put: measured through a long scroll it holds its header position, `view-transition-name: none`, outside `main`. Everything under that sentence is a 2025 studio template. Four equal pastel cards, a numbered services grid, "Three steps. No theater.", a founder stat row, an FAQ, a closing card. `/services` and `/pricing` reuse the same eyebrow, extrabold display, rounded cards, and violet primary. The product character that should carry the headline leaks into a GitHub commit ticker and stack names (Claude, Composio, MCP). Trucks and kitchens never become a layout, a proof, or a picture.

The new light helps. Page changes dim and lift `main` only. One thin lamp moves with scroll. Sections pick up a hairline and a soft shadow. Reduced motion removes them. The lockup does not travel, fade, or morph. What hurts is the hero, where a typewriter deletes the specific line while the ticker rotates and three green dots pulse. The lamp is quiet. The sentence is not.

## Overall Impression

The voice at the top is the product. The first viewport spends that voice on a commit pill, a deleting typewriter, and four equal cards, then lets the cookie sit on the last card while the price and both commercial buttons wait below the fold. The single biggest opportunity is to put headline, diagnostic, price, and the two actions in the first screen, and let the mark stay the still point it already is.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The first screen hides the sale, and the cookie sits on the offer.**
Why it matters: At 1440×900 the hero is `min-h-[min(92vh,900px)]`, so the price and both hero buttons fall below 900px. The cookie covers Growth engineering on desktop and the AI agents card on a 390×844 phone. After a short scroll, mobile stacks a sticky Book a call on the hero's own Book a call. Jordan and Casey cannot complete one action without first fighting chrome.
Fix: Shorten the hero so headline, diagnostic, price, and the two buttons share the first viewport. Move the four cards down. Keep the cookie off the cards. Show the sticky bar only after the hero buttons have left the screen.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] The menu undoes the headline.**
Why it matters: Hero cards and the services grid set Brand systems and Growth engineering beside dispatch, then talk in Claude, Composio, MCP, and Code Connect. A kitchen owner cannot tell what is for sale.
Fix: Make the four offers operator loops (dispatch, paperwork, the tool that does not exist, invoices). Leave the stack one step down on the service page.
Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] The proof is a commit log.**
Why it matters: The ticker is the first evidence on the page. Founder stats are a year, a city, and "4 Service pillars." Riley is comparing agencies and never sees an operator result.
Fix: Replace the ticker with one true, anonymized outcome in the headline block. Drop "4 Service pillars" as a stat.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] "Get a price estimate" is not an estimate.**
Why it matters: The button opens a Typeform. The homepage only explains that in the FAQ. `/pricing` says "Clear bands" while one tier is "Scoped sprint" and the main tier is still a 6× range. The cookie covers the disclaimer and both CTAs.
Fix: Rename the button to the action ("Send the job. I'll reply with a band."). Put a band or "quoted after the call" on Single workflow, and place the disclaimer next to the buttons.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The typewriter spends the one specific sentence.**
Why it matters: The logo correctly stays still. The lamp and the route wash are short and honor reduced motion. The typewriter deletes "dispatch that actually dispatches" while it is being read, in parallel with the ticker.
Fix: Set one static line under the headline. Keep the lamp.
Suggested command: `/impeccable quieter`
