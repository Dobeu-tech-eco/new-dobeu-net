## Product overview
**One-liner:** We build usable business technology.
Dobeu is Jeremy Williams's home for passion projects, useful experiments and collaboration with like-minded people. The portfolio spans practical software, comics, publishing and art commerce.
**Category/type:** independent personal project business; product portfolio and collaboration invitation. Do not market it as a consulting firm, AI/agent builder or staffed technology agency.
**Business model:** projects and partnerships; optional scoped paid builds exist on /pricing. Do not imply every conversation is a sales lead, promise equity terms, or infer product revenue.

## Target audience
People who see a useful idea, care about making it real and want to explore working together. Specific industries, organization sizes and buying roles are not established. The products have distinct audiences; do not collapse them into one SaaS customer profile.
**Jobs to be done (hypotheses):** see what Jeremy makes; find a related interest; start a concrete conversation about a possible collaboration.
**Personas:** no validated B2B purchasing committee. Avoid invented personas.

## Problems and pain points
Owner-observed positioning problem: technology agencies are common and often feel impersonal. Dobeu should convey the individual and real projects behind the work.
Visitor problems, cost of inaction and emotional pain have not been validated by customer interviews. Do not invent numbers or quotes.

## Competitive landscape
No direct competitors nominated. Research comparable independent product studios and maker portfolios for presentation and collaboration patterns; label them analogues, not proven commercial rivals. Do not infer competitor weaknesses from omissions.

## Differentiation
Personal involvement, a varied portfolio, curiosity and practical usefulness are the intended differentiators. Portfolio links demonstrate subject matter and output, not paid-client outcomes or superiority. Customer reasons for choosing Dobeu are unverified.

## Objections and switching dynamics
Hypotheses: visitors may be unclear what kind of collaboration fits, whether a budget is required, and what happens after contacting Jeremy. Answer through a specific invitation and accurate form feedback; do not promise response times or free work.
No validated switching journey; treat this as a first conversation rather than replacement of a vendor. Poor fit: people expecting a staffed agency or guaranteed delivery before an agreed scope.

## Customer language
Customer quotes: unavailable.
Owner language: “We build usable business technology.” “passion projects” “like-minded” “Purposely keep me vague”.
Use: useful, make, build, explore, projects, collaboration.
Avoid in public positioning: AI, agents, consulting, unsupported credentials, invented customer metrics, career details, personal location and private repo names. Internal identifiers and developer tooling are unaffected.

## Brand voice
| Attribute | Means | Does not mean | Existing example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | Jeremy speaks plainly as an individual | Invent a team | “I’m Jeremy.” |
| Practical | Concrete work and usable outcomes | Broad performance guarantees | “We build usable business technology.” |
| Curious | Invite a conversation about ideas | Promise every idea becomes a project | “Explore a collaboration” |
| Understated | Keep bio broad | Hide product identity or contact purpose | “Passion projects & collaboration.” |

Support/errors: direct explanation and next action. Launch: specific projects and invitation. Social: first person, no invented autobiography. Legal/billing: precision takes precedence over promotional tone.
Voice is derived from user direction and current copy, not measured campaign performance.
