# AI Consultant NYC

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** AI automation consulting for NYC-based businesses
- **What they do:** Replace manual workflows with AI systems that save 20–40 hours weekly; deliver in 30 days without long-term contracts
- **Target audience:** Professional services, sales orgs, healthcare, finance, real estate firms in NYC metro paying $80K+ salaries for repetitive work

**Notable patterns:** 
- **Alternating image placement:** Service cards flip screenshot/icon side L→R→L. Pricing tiers use 2-column uniform grid with navy background break. FAQs are accordion disclosure pattern (collapsed by default). Color alternation: beige hero/body sections contrast with navy background on pricing and footer sections. One **uniform 4-cell grid** for service cards (equal width, stacked on mobile). **Masonry 2-column grid** for pricing tiers (4 cells, 2×2). No masonry varying-height columns; all grids are either uniform or fixed-span bento. Sticky header with orange CTA button; phone link inline. All controls use rounded corners; pill shapes on buttons. Interaction: :focus-visible present on form inputs and links; prefers-reduced-motion honored (no auto-scroll or parallax).
