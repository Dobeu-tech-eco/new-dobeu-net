# Step 7 — Launch strategy

Installed launch 2.2.0 and site-launch-qa reference. Inputs: end-state Topology/criteria, scout surface, confirmed marketing context, steps 2–6. This is a rebrand of an existing personal portfolio, not a new SaaS launch. No publishing, outreach, scheduling or production action performed.

## Readiness gate
Simple: yes for the intended job, see real projects and start a collaboration conversation.
Lovable: unverified; no fresh visitor research. Do not infer demand from clean copy.
Complete: not yet demonstrated on the current release candidate. Resolve public offer inconsistency, privacy wording and rendered/form verification before announcing the rebrand as complete.

## Prioritized launch sequence
| Stage | Work | Owner | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Reconcile optional package offer and service framing; confirm copy/metadata scope | Jeremy + implementation owner | Approved bounded plan and diff |
| Local QA | Verify forms with mocks, durable acceptance, project links, mobile/keyboard flow, metadata and consent | Verifier | Fresh tests and rendered walkthrough on candidate |
| Release review | Review receipt and remaining live checks | Jeremy | Explicit ship/deploy decision; no deployment implied here |
| Production check | After authorized deployment, inspect candidate commit's live pages and authorized intake delivery | Verifier + Jeremy | Deployment identity and screen/form evidence |
| Share | Small personal introduction pointing to portfolio and collaboration invitation | Jeremy | Approved post; publish separately |
| Learn | Review inquiries and project interest over a matching closed period | Jeremy | Observed results and specific follow-up decision |

## ORB channels
Owned: dobeu.net portfolio first. Use existing publications only where the topic fits; do not establish a new mailing list for this audit.
Rented: optional personal LinkedIn post linking to the portfolio, authored/approved by Jeremy. Do not use old posts or follows to infer new credentials.
Borrowed: individual collaborators can share a relevant project when they choose. No unsolicited outreach or partner promise.

## Announcement draft (not sent)
“Dobeu is the home for my passion projects and useful experiments. Explore RouteReady, Chima Comics, Dobeu.space, and Ikram & Co. If you see an idea you’d like to explore together, I’d like to hear from you.”
Add the approved site URL after the release candidate is verified live. Avoid saying the rebrand has launched before deployment evidence exists.

## Deliberate omissions
Product Hunt, waitlists, discounts, paid ads, affiliate programs, automated nurture and mass outreach are unnecessary to this bounded rebrand. No new feature should delay a complete portfolio/contact experience.

## Before/after release checks
Before: public copy/links, legal/processing alignment, safe form tests, consent, unique page metadata, current build/browser evidence and rollback SHA. At release: require explicit authority, record deployed SHA, check canonical/index/social output and agreed live conversion checks. After: compare same closed windows, log visitor feedback and correct actual defects. No dates, response deadlines or numeric growth promises invented.
